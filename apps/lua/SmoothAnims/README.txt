An Assetto Corsa Lua App that visually smoothens out steering and shifting animations, without affecting actual steering and input values.

How to Install

Method 1: Content Manager
Drag and drop the .zip archive directly into Content Manager and click Install.

Method 2: Manual Installation
Extract the apps folder from the archive directly into your Assetto Corsa root directory.



Configuration & Settings

- Smoothness (default: 8.0): Adjusts steering damping speed. Lower values will give a heavier feel, while higher values will feel more direct. Feel free to tweak based on your preferences.
- Smooth shift animation: Applies smoothing to the gear shifter reach and return movements.
- Shift smoothness: Speed of the shifting arm movement.
- Preload hand on redline: Prepares the driver's hand on the shifter when nearing the rev limiter in manual cars.
- Shifter rest mode: Keeps the driver's hand resting on the shifter knob for 5 seconds in 3rd gear+, adding +3 seconds for every subsequent upshift.
